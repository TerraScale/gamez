# Party AI: performance and balance (2026-10-28)

Measurements for the classic party AI (docs/design/party.md §6, §8, §12; task T-auto). They
cover what the leader and follower brains cost per sim tick, whether 4 heroes and 300 monsters
fit the 2 ms budget, and where a party of four lands against the §8 balance band.

**Caveat first.** The host is the shared 46-vCPU Xeon E5-2660 v4 VM (Node 24) used by the other
October benches. Other agents were building and running tests at the same time (load average
2–22 during these runs). Read the numbers as relative. The test assertions allow 3× the budget
for this reason.

## Integration update: `World.peek()` (ADR 0007 §6)

After the builders' numbers below were taken, the integrator profiled the 4-hero scenario. The
kernel's rollback copies of live reads (`copyStored`) took 26 % of a tick. The new read-only
`world.peek()` in the hot loops, plus skipping no-op stat merges, gave these results (plain node,
one `sim.step(1)` per tick, load average about 5):

| Scenario (300 awake monsters)                       | Before peek | After peek |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ----------: | ---------: |
| `party.perf` scenario (core sim), 1 hero            |     2.43 ms |    1.24 ms |
| `party.perf` scenario (core sim), 4 heroes          |     3.26 ms |    1.78 ms |
| `party.auto.perf` scenario (every module), 4 heroes |      4.4 ms |     2.6 ms |

- The core sim now meets the 2 ms budget with 4 heroes.
- With every module, the sustained 300-monster melee is about 2.3 ms for the best window and
  2.6 ms mean. The party AI's share is unchanged at about 0.5 ms.
- `xpSharePct` now ships as the proposal below, [100, 65, 54, 48].
- Perf tests assert against a load-scaled budget (`modules/classic/tests/support/perf.ts`).

`tools/bench/src/party.ts` after peek (load average about 22 from other workflows, so the means
are noisy and the best windows are the better guide):

| Case                                           | Mean ms/tick | Best 25-tick ms | Leader think ms | Followers ms |
| ---------------------------------------------- | -----------: | --------------: | --------------: | -----------: |
| solo hero + 300 monsters                       |        3.179 |           2.091 |           0.258 |        0.001 |
| 4 heroes + 300 monsters                        |        3.152 |           2.397 |           0.245 |        0.271 |
| 4 heroes + 300 monsters, party brains disabled |        1.913 |           1.805 |               — |            — |

Normal Idle play from town (6000 ticks) costs 0.75 ms per tick solo and 1.18 ms with 4 heroes.

## 1. Cost per tick

### Worst-case fight

The setup is the full classic mode (every module, real content). Four recruited heroes are on
Full Idle in their first dungeon level, topped up to 300 awake monsters within 4–30 tiles. The
monsters' life is raised so none die, and the heroes are healed outside the timed step so
nobody leaves. One `sim.step(1)` per tick, over 400 ticks.

- The **think / followers / allocate** columns are the wall time inside `classic.auto.think`
  (the leader brain), `classic.auto.party` (every follower) and `classic.auto.allocate`. Kernel
  `wrap` overrides time them, which changes no behaviour. This is the AI's own cost.
- The **brains disabled** row restores the same world with both brains switched off. The heroes
  then stand idle, so that row also drops all the combat the heroes cause. The difference from
  the four-hero row is an upper bound of the AI's cost, not the cost itself.

Final build, load average 3–7:

| Case                                           | Heroes | Monsters | Mean ms/tick | Best 25-tick ms | Leader think ms | Followers ms | Allocate ms |
| ---------------------------------------------- | -----: | -------: | -----------: | --------------: | --------------: | -----------: | ----------: |
| solo hero + 300 monsters                       |      1 |      300 |        3.622 |           3.459 |           0.278 |        0.001 |       0.002 |
| 4 heroes + 300 monsters                        |      4 |      300 |        3.915 |           3.508 |           0.282 |        0.220 |       0.003 |
| 4 heroes + 300 monsters, party brains disabled |      4 |      300 |        3.160 |           2.940 |               — |            — |           — |

- **The party AI costs 0.50 ms/tick:**
  - The leader is 0.28 ms. It is the solo controller, unchanged, and costs the same as the solo
    hero's.
  - The three followers are 0.22 ms together, about 0.07 ms each.
  - `tests/party.auto.perf.test.ts` measured 0.63–0.68 ms under heavier load. It asserts the AI
    stays under 1 ms and the best 25-tick window under 6 ms (3× the budget).
- **Follower optimisations.** The first build's followers cost 0.67 ms (0.22 ms each), because
  they rescanned the full 12-tile radius on every fight tick. Three changes brought that to
  0.07 ms each:
  1. No rescan while a skill intent on a living focus is still running.
  2. Full scans only on re-plans (every 8 ticks, staggered by slot), with a 10-tile follower
     radius.
  3. Between re-plans, a 4-tile scan plus the focus foe, which is enough for AoE counts and
     kiting.

  Cheap life checks also run before skill lists and member contexts are built. These changes
  made the party stronger, not weaker (section 3).

- **The 2 ms budget is not met by the sim itself.** With every module and 300 awake monsters
  converging, the step costs 2.9–4.3 ms without any AI. That cost is in core, combat, monsters
  and world (task T-core; see `tests/party.perf.test.ts`). The solo hero costs about as much as
  the party in this fight.
- **CPU profile.** The largest single item is the monster AI (`monsters/think.ts`). In the
  party AI, `scanFoes` (grid query plus line of sight per foe) dominates.
- **Flakiness.** Inside the full parallel classic suite, every perf test fails on its mean (core
  movement, T-core's party perf, and this one before its assertion moved to the best window and
  the AI time).

## 2. Soak: 4 heroes, Full Idle, 40 000 ticks

`tests/party.idle.test.ts` runs a warrior, rogue, sorcerer and warrior, seed 2026.

| Run                             | Deepest | Kills | Deaths | Levels      | In leash | Worst far run | Town ticks | Downed ticks |
| ------------------------------- | ------: | ----: | -----: | ----------- | -------: | ------------: | ---------: | -----------: |
| first build                     |       8 |   843 |      4 | 13/13/13/13 |    100 % |           201 |       5648 |          236 |
| + perf, town leash              |       7 |   874 |      0 | 13/13/13/13 |    100 % |           201 |       6999 |            0 |
| + town gather fix, focus fire   |       8 |   819 |     15 | 14/14/13/14 |    100 % |           201 |       1947 |         2559 |
| + narrow follower scans (final) |       8 |   862 |      1 | 14/14/14/14 |    100 % |           201 |       1646 |         1953 |

- **In leash** is the share of sampled ticks with every follower within
  `PartyProto.leashTeleport` of the acting leader.
- **Worst far run** is the longest run of ticks with a follower more than 6 tiles away, outside
  town and fights. The 200-tick leash regroup caps it at 201.
- The **town gather fix** cut time in town from 17 % to 5 % of the run.
  - Before it, the leader waited for followers to stand within 4 tiles. Formation spots lie up
    to `2 × spread` tiles away (plus the walkable-tile search), so almost every town visit
    waited the full 600-tick cap.
  - The gather radius is now `max(4, 2 × spread + 2)`.
- Deaths and downed ticks vary a lot between builds and runs. The final build died once in
  40 000 ticks.

## 3. Balance against the §8 band

The test (`tests/party.balance.test.ts`) uses real content on Auto, with `maxDepth` 4 and the
same ticks for both sides.

- **Party:** a warrior founder plus rogue, sorcerer and warrior recruits, all level 1 (recruits
  start at the highest level − 3 = 1). The purse starts with the founder's starter gold.
- **Solo baseline:** one run per party class, averaged with the party's weights (the warrior
  counts twice).
- **XP per hero:** XP is split into kill XP and quest XP. Quest rewards go in full to every
  standing member, which is T-progress's rule, not this task's.

### Default `PartyProto`

The default was `xpSharePct` [100, 75, 62, 55] (now [100, 65, 54, 48], see the update at the top).

| 2026 |  15 |            1.57 |              1.78 |          1.72 |                       0 |             0 | + gather fix, focus fire |

|    7 |  15 |            1.50 |              1.67 |          1.60 |                       0 |             0 | final                    |

The CI check (`tests/party.balance.test.ts`) uses the mean of seeds 2026 and 7, because one seed
is too noisy for a band. On the final build that mean is:

- **kills 1.57×**
- **XP 1.815×**, which is **outside the band** (at most 1.8×)
- deaths 0 for both the party and solo

So the band test **fails with the shipped defaults**, on XP alone. The cause is the follower
fixes: the town gather radius, focus fire reaching past the assist radius, and the narrower
scans made the party kill faster, and it now levels into the top of the band.

Single seeds vary in both directions. Three things drive the spread:

1. Whether the party reaches depth 4 early.
2. Rare champion packs.
3. Time spent in town.

### Proposals

All proposals were measured through `BAL_PARTY` with the final build, 15 minutes, against the
same solo baselines. `rules.ts` is the architect's file and was not changed.

| `xpSharePct`                     | Seed | Kills/min ratio | XP/hero/min ratio | Kill-XP ratio |
| -------------------------------- | ---: | --------------: | ----------------: | ------------: |
| [100, 65, 54, 48] (**proposed**) | 2026 |            1.40 |              1.63 |          1.59 |
| [100, 65, 54, 48] (**proposed**) |    7 |            1.45 |              1.64 |          1.46 |
| [100, 60, 48, 42]                | 2026 |            1.23 |              1.33 |          1.24 |
| [100, 60, 48, 42]                |    7 |            1.25 |              0.92 |          0.96 |
| [100, 60, 48, 42]                | 4242 |            1.16 |              1.41 |          1.27 |

**Proposal: set `DEFAULT_PARTY_PROTO.xpSharePct` to [100, 65, 54, 48].** On the CI seeds the
mean is kills 1.43× and XP 1.64×, both inside the band with room on either side. With 48 %,
each member of a party of four gets 2.10 × 0.48 = 1.01× a solo kill's XP. The rest of the
XP-per-hero gain comes from the party killing faster.

The sharper cut, [100, 60, 48, 42], slows levelling so much that the kill ratio falls to the
band's lower edge (1.16–1.25). Sizes 2 and 3 are scaled by the same factor (about 0.87) and
were not measured separately.

### Not measured here

The balance of sizes 2 and 3, depths beyond 4, and levels above about 10 are not measured. The
band test is capped at depth 4 and 15 minutes so that CI stays near two minutes.
